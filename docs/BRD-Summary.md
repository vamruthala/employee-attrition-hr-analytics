# Business Requirements Summary

- **Business question:** I asked where historical attrition rates differ across employee groups.
- **Decision supported:** My analysis uses within-group rates and exit counts to focus retention investigations.
- **Scope:** Comparisons cover **1,470** employee records across role, department, satisfaction, travel, and salary-increase views.
- **Success measures:** Rates are calculated attrition with each group’s employee count as the denominator and review rates alongside exit counts.
