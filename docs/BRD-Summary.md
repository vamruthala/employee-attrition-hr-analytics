# Business Requirements Summary

*Condensed from the project report; not a formally approved BRD.*

- **Business question:** Where do historical attrition rates differ across employee groups?
- **Decision supported:** Prioritize retention investigation using within-group rates and exit counts.
- **Scope:** 1,470 employee records; role, department, satisfaction, travel, and salary-increase views.
- **Success measures:** Correctly defined attrition denominators; reconcile salary-chart labels before compensation use.
- **Stakeholders:** Specific sponsor and HR decision roles are not named in the source.
